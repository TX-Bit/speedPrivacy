Speed Only

Privacy Policy

Speed Only is a simple GPS speedometer for iPhone and Apple Watch. We designed it to be private by default.

Summary

Speed Only does not require an account, does not show ads, and does not track you.

  • No account required
  • No advertising
  • No third-party tracking
  • No sale of personal data
  • Location is used only to calculate speed and distance

Information We Collect

Speed Only does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, contacts, photos, or account details.

The app may access your device location only when you allow it. Location access is used to calculate your current GPS speed, distance, and related trip information.

Location Data

Speed Only uses location data to provide speedometer functionality.

  • Current speed
  • Maximum speed
  • Average speed
  • Distance
  • Trip duration

Location data is processed on your device. In the current version, Speed Only does not upload your location data to our servers.

Data Storage

Speed Only may store simple app preferences locally on your device, such as your selected speed unit, display options, and app mode.

If trip history is added in a future version, trip summaries may be stored locally on your device unless otherwise stated.

Third-Party Services

Speed Only does not use third-party advertising or analytics services in the current version.

Apple Services

Speed Only runs on Apple platforms and may use system services provided by Apple, such as Core Location. Your use of Apple services is also subject to Apple’s own privacy practices.

Children’s Privacy

Speed Only does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will update the “Last updated” date below.
